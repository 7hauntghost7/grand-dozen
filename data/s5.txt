DECK: Set 5 — Q95–154 · haematology & coagulation paper

Q: Hemolytic anemia is due to all of the following except:
NO: 95
A. Malaria
B. Sickle cell disease
C. Spheroctosis
D. Rhesus incompatibility
E. Iron deficiency
ANSWER: E
EXPLANATION: Iron deficiency is a hypoproliferative (microcytic) anaemia, not a haemolytic one.

Q: Leucopenia:
NO: 96
A. Reduction in white blood cell count
B. Caused by acute bacterial infections
C. Caused by Vitamin B12 deficiency
D. Is normally seen in children
E. Might involve the granular cells
ANSWER: A, C, E
EXPLANATION: Leucopenia = reduced WBC count (B12 deficiency can cause it; neutropenia involves granulocytes). Acute bacterial infection causes leukocytosis.
NOTE: A is the definition; C and E are also true.

Q: The following is a function of neutrophils:

A. Phagocytosis
B. Antibody production
C. Cell mediate immunity
D. Humoral immunity
E. Complement activation
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Neutrophils are phagocytes.

Q: The following are true about T-Lymphocytes except:

Q: Hemophilia is caused by:
NO: 100

Q: The bleeding time:
NO: 102
A. Normally equals 15-20 minutes
B. Is abnormal in thrombocytopenia
C. Is abnormal in hemophilia
D. Is abnormal in liver failure

EXPLANATION: BT tests platelets/vessels: normal 2–6 min; prolonged in thrombocytopenia; normal in haemophilia.
NOTE: E is partly true (BT reflects platelet + vascular phase), but B is the cleanest answer.

Q: All of the following may be a complication of blood transfusion, except:

Q: Plasma:
NO: 118
A. Is 85% water
B. Contains about 5g protein/dL
C. Contains 290 mosmol/liter
D. Volume can be measured by inulin dilution method
E. Constitute about 45% of the blood
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Plasma osmolarity ≈ 285–295 mOsm/L. It’s ~92% water, ~7 g protein/dL, ~55% of blood; plasma volume uses Evans blue/125I-albumin.

Q: Compared to other plasma proteins, albumin is the main generator of the colloid osmotic pressure of plasma because:
NO: 119

B. It can bind other substances in blood
C. It can relatively easily cross the capillary membrane
D. It is not ionized
E. It can be made by the liver
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Oncotic pressure depends on the NUMBER of molecules: albumin is abundant and small.

Q: Normal red blood cells:
NO: 121
A. Rupture if suspended in a solution which is 0.9% sodium chloride
B. Are more fragile than sphercytes
C. Count is about 5 million/mm3 in adult females
D. Count about 40 picograms of hemoglobin per cell
E. Volume is about 85 femtoliters
ANSWER: E
EXPLANATION: MCV ≈ 80–100 fL. 0.9% saline is isotonic; spherocytes are more fragile; female count ≈ 4.5 million; MCH ≈ 30 pg.
NOTE: C (5 million) is borderline; E is the cleanest.

Q: Active immunity:
NO: 130

B. Does not develop unless a foreign organism or toxin invade the body

ANSWER: A, B
EXPLANATION: Active immunity requires exposure to an antigen (usually MW > 8000). Milk/placental antibodies are passive.

Q: Which of the following is not a function of antibodies:

EXPLANATION: Attracting WBCs is done by complement fragments/cytokines, not by antibodies directly.
