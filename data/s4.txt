Q: Neutrophils:
NO: 27
A. contains heparin.
B. are produced mainly in the spleen in adults.
C. can produce hydrogen peroxide.
D. have life span of few days.
E. increases in viral infections.
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Respiratory burst generates H2O2/superoxide. Heparin is in basophils/mast cells; adult production is in marrow; neutrophilia is typical of bacterial infection.
NOTE: D is loosely true too (hours in blood, days in tissue) but C is the classic key.

Q: Red blood cell (RBC):
NO: 28
A. have few organelles.
B. in adults are produced in the spleen & bone marrow.
C. their size increases in iron deficiency anaemia.
D. their mean haemoglobin concentration increases in folic acid deficiency.
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Mature RBCs have no nucleus or mitochondria. In adults they’re made only in marrow; iron deficiency makes them smaller (microcytic).
NOTE: Source option c was missing; remaining shown as A–D.

Q: The following is true about iron:
NO: 29
A. it is mainly absorbed in the duodenum.
B. daily loss in males is less than 1 mg.
C. serum ferritin is a good index of iron storage.

ANSWER: A, C
EXPLANATION: Absorbed mainly in duodenum/upper jejunum; serum ferritin reflects stores. Milk is a poor source; vitamin C INCREASES absorption.
NOTE: B (~1 mg/day loss) is roughly right too.

Q: Hb S:
